The renowned steakhouse La Cabrera continues to consolidate its international presence with the official opening of its third restaurant in Miami, this time in the vibrant Midtown neighborhood. May 1st marks a special date for food lovers, who can now enjoy authentic Argentine grilled meats in a new space that blends tradition, elegance, and flavor.

Prior to the grand opening, Miamix guests were treated to an exclusive experience that offered a complete tour of the restaurant's signature flavors. Among them, Cristhian Gassmann stood out as a true asado enthusiast, delighting in every cut, garnish, and detail that makes La Cabrera unique. From the flank steak and ribeye to the classic sides served in small casseroles, every dish was left untasted.

From Buenos Aires to the world, Gastón Riveira, founder and soul of La Cabrera, has managed to position his restaurant as one of the most successful in Argentina. With a vision distinct from that of the typical grill chef, Riveira is involved in every aspect: product selection, cost analysis, trend research, and, above all, rigorous tasting. His mission: to preserve the spirit of Argentine family food with a creative and modern approach.

The new location at 3250 Buena Vista Blvd #124, Midtown Miami, joins the already established locations in Sunny Isles Beach and Coconut Grove, offering residents and visitors of South Florida a gastronomic experience that honors the culture of the Río de la Plata, in a sophisticated yet welcoming atmosphere.

In addition to its presence in Argentina and the United States, La Cabrera has expanded its concept globally, with restaurants in Chile, Colombia, Mexico, Peru, Paraguay, the Philippines, and Spain, bringing its unique grilling style to diners from different cultures. Each location maintains the original essence, but adapts to the local environment and tastes, without losing authenticity.

The brand has also reached major international airports, bringing its flavors to travelers around the world. Its airport terminal locations offer customized versions of its signature dishes, allowing the La Cabrera experience to be enjoyed even while in transit.

With each new opening, La Cabrera reaffirms its commitment to quality, passion for meat, and hospitality. The experience revolves not only around flavor, but also family values, respect for tradition, and dedication to the details that transform a meal into an unforgettable memory.
