Inter Miami CF [@intermiamicf](https://www.instagram.com/intermiamicf/) brought the party to Wynwood with "Euphoria Begins," an event that marked the start of the season and also served as the official unveiling of the "Euphoria" uniform. In collaboration with Royal Caribbean, the club turned the afternoon into a celebration open to all its fans.

The big surprise was a new mural in Wynwood, a stunning work of art created by JEKS [@jeks_nc](https://www.instagram.com/jeks_nc/?hl=en), a renowned graffiti artist and muralist. The piece depicts Inter Miami CF players wearing the new jersey and joins the neighborhood's iconic murals, adding a soccer-inspired touch to the city's street art.

Just days before the start of the 2025 season, hundreds of fans gathered to share their memories with the team. Among the players in attendance were Fafa Picault [@fafapicault](https://www.instagram.com/fafapicault/?hl=en), one of the recent signings, and Benjamin Cremaschi [@benjacremaschi](https://www.instagram.com/benjacremaschi/?hl=en), a local talent who couldn't hide his excitement upon seeing his face on the mural.

“It's incredible to be here. I was born and raised in Miami… Seeing my face on a mural in such an iconic part of the city is a dream. The jersey is spectacular; the pink represents Miami so well,” Cremaschi said.

For his part, Picault was also excited: “Growing up in South Miami, I've seen how Wynwood has evolved over time. I've been coming here since I was a teenager, so seeing my face on a mural is something very special and emotional. The 'Euphoria' uniform captures the style, attitude, and flavor of the city and its people. I think it's a beautiful jersey.”

With this event, Inter Miami CF not only ignited the enthusiasm of its fans, but also made it clear that the new season arrives with all the energy, artistry, and passion that characterize the club and the city.
