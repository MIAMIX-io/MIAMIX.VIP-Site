The Argentine Consulate General and Promotion Center in Miami hosted a special event where journalist and writer Valeria Schapira shared her latest book with the public. The event brought together readers, friends, and literature lovers in a relaxed and highly participatory atmosphere.

<!-- SINGLE IMAGE -->
![Valeria Schapira](/images/culture/vva13-1024x709.jpg)

With her approachable and charismatic style, Valeria spoke about the spirit of her new work, which invites reflection and self-discovery. Known for her ability to connect with people's emotions, the author also shared anecdotes from her creative process and encouraged those present to view life's challenges with optimism and resilience.

The event was produced by Silvina Fuentes, who has promoted numerous cultural gatherings in Miami that bring the Argentine and Latin American communities closer to literary, artistic, and musical figures. Her collaboration with the consulate allowed for an intimate and intimate space between the author and the audience.

{% include video.html url="https://www.youtube.com/embed/fNS5gAX4KoM?feature=oembed" caption="Valeria Schapira" %}

During the presentation, attendees enjoyed a reading of excerpts from the book by Valeria, as well as an open discussion with questions and answers that gave the event a personal and authentic touch. Book signings and photo ops were also part of the event, which concluded with smiles and applause.

The Argentine Consulate once again became a meeting point for celebrating national culture and talent, reaffirming its commitment to promoting literary and artistic offerings that strengthen the connection between the South Florida community and the Argentine identity.

At MIAMIX, we celebrate Valeria Schapira's presence in Miami, the work of Silvina Fuentes, and the support of the Argentine Consulate. These types of initiatives enrich the city's cultural calendar and offer the opportunity to discover books that inspire, excite, and encourage us to continue growing.
