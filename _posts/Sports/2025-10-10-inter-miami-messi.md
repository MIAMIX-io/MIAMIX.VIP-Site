As a spectator and supporter of Inter Miami, experiencing this stage of the team's career has been a roller coaster of emotions. Through Miamix's media coverage, with Silvina Fuentes and Cristhian Gassmann, we were able to feel the pulse of each match, the unwavering support of the fans, and the excitement ignited by every goal.

The match against Jamaica's Cavaliers was one of those games where the atmosphere was festive, the stadium buzzing, and everything seemed to be going in Inter's favor. The team won clearly, and the atmosphere was optimistic. The fans, many of them carrying flags, personalized jerseys, and even shed tears of emotion, experienced a magical day that reinforced their connection with the team.
