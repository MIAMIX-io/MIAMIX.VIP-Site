Tulum is a destination meant to be experienced through the senses. We’ve visited it many times, always searching for ways to connect with nature, to feel the jungle, the sea, and the freedom that only this corner of the Mexican Caribbean can offer. Each trip reminds us that in Tulum it’s not just about arriving, but about how you move, because along that journey lie the true pleasures of the destination.

Getting around Tulum is, without a doubt, the best-kept secret to discovering its wonders. Hidden beaches, dirt roads winding through the jungle, secret cenotes, and local spots are not explored the same way by taxi or conventional transportation. That’s where Jungle Rides Tulum makes all the difference, offering an authentic, practical, and adventurous way to experience paradise.

Jungle Rides is a local company based in Aldea Zama, created to provide a smart and safe solution for mobility in Tulum. Their concept goes far beyond simple vehicle rentals, it’s an invitation to experience the destination freely, adapting to each traveler’s rhythm and the type of experience they want to live.

For those seeking adrenaline and full contact with nature, Jungle Rides’ ATVs are ideal for exploring jungle trails, sandy roads, and lesser-known areas. It’s the perfect option for a day of adventure, exploration, and discovery, where every ride becomes part of the memory.

If the idea is to cruise along the coast, visit beach clubs, nearby cenotes, or simply move around in style and comfort, scooters are an excellent alternative. Agile, practical, and fun, they allow you to enjoy the scenery without rushing, feeling the breeze and the freedom that defines Tulum.

For travelers who prefer a more relaxed and eco-friendly pace, Jungle Rides also offers bicycles, ideal for getting around Aldea Zama, running errands, discovering cafés, restaurants, and points of interest, or simply enjoying the natural surroundings in a conscious and sustainable way.

One of Jungle Rides’ greatest advantages is the ability to create flexible packages, switching between different mobility options throughout your stay. One day on a scooter, another on an ATV, and another by bike a perfect solution for those who want to experience Tulum from different perspectives without complications.

Behind Jungle Rides is Kevin and his family, who have built a community rather than just a business.

“I truly strive to give you all the information and security you need for a beautiful experience. You’re not just renting a vehicle - you’re joining our community and discovering Tulum through local eyes,”

Kevin shares, reflecting the brand’s warm and authentic spirit.

Located on Calle Itzamna in Aldea Zama, Jungle Rides invites you to feel the freedom and live the journey with its philosophy FEEL THE FREEDOM – LIVE THE RIDE. Exploring hidden cenotes, jungle paths, and pristine beaches is all part of the experience. Because in Tulum, the adventure doesn’t begin when you arrive - it begins when you choose how to explore it.
