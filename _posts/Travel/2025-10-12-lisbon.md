If you have a layover in Lisbon, our advice is clear: stay 2 or 3 nights to discover one of the most beautiful cities in Europe. Walking through its cobblestone streets that turn into stairways is an experience that transports you across centuries of history. The Portuguese capital perfectly blends its antiquity with a vibrant contemporary scene, and the warmth of the people makes you feel at home from the first moment.

Must‑see spots include the Alfama neighborhood with its spectacular viewpoints, the Belém Tower, the Jerónimos Monastery, and of course, a ride on the iconic tram 28. Do not miss São Jorge Castle for the best panoramic city views, or the modern Parque das Nações district, which showcases Lisbon’s contemporary side with striking architecture.

For food lovers, eating and drinking is an essential part of the itinerary. Freshly baked pastéis de nata at the legendary Pastéis de Belém are a must, as is tasting cod in its many versions. Pair your meals with a good vinho verde, a Douro red, or try ginjinha, the typical cherry liqueur often served in chocolate cups at traditional downtown bars.

For lodging, Lisbon offers options for every taste. In Alfama, the oldest neighborhood, we recommend the Memmo Alfama Hotel with spectacular views of the Tagus River, or the boutique Santiago de Alfama for a more intimate experience. In Bairro Alto, the Bairro Alto Hotel combines contemporary design with a prime location, while in Chiado, the Almada Noire Boutique Hotel offers elegance and proximity to the best shops and restaurants.
