Located in the heart of Cachi, in the province of Salta, Argentina, El Cortijo Hotel Boutique is much more than just accommodation. It is a sensory experience that transports its guests to the most authentic essence of the Calchaquí Valleys, where time seems to have stopped in the year 1700.

From the first moment you cross the door of this old village house, you are enveloped by a unique atmosphere. The warm colors of the adobe walls, the aromas of the patio flowers, and the textures of stone and wood create an environment of absolute relaxation. The exquisite collection of art and ancient imagery that decorates every corner of the hotel adds a touch of distinction and culture that makes each space a living museum.

The hotel's architecture faithfully respects the area's construction traditions. Built with typical materials such as stone, adobe, cane, and wood, the building integrates perfectly with its surroundings, creating a visual harmony that invites contemplation and rest. Each room is unique, with its own personality and charm, offering cozy environments where comfort meets authenticity.

One of El Cortijo's great attractions is its privileged location. Cachi is a village that preserves its intact colonial architecture, surrounded by impressive geography that includes imposing mountains, green valleys, and a deep blue sky that seems infinite. The landscape, cultural, and architectural richness of the place turns every walk through its cobblestone streets into a journey to the past.

The gastronomic experience at El Cortijo deserves a chapter of its own. The exclusive Catalino Restaurant, led by the talented Chef Juanjo Robles, offers a culinary proposal that combines the best of northern tradition with a contemporary gourmet touch. The dishes, prepared with top-quality local products, are true works of art that delight both the eye and the palate.

Juanjo Robles is passionate about traditional Argentine cuisine, especially humita and asado. His culinary philosophy is based on respecting ancestral recipes while adding his personal touch and refined technique. Each preparation is a tribute to the authentic flavors of Salta, presented with modern aesthetics that pleasantly surprises diners.

The hotel's hosts, Gustavo Bergesi and Omar Farhat, are the soul of this project. Their personalized attention and warm hospitality make each guest feel at home, but in an extraordinary home. They know every secret of Cachi and are always willing to share recommendations and stories that enrich the stay.

El Cortijo also has a charming artisan shop where you can purchase unique pieces created by local craftspeople. From Andean textiles to ceramics and silverwork, each object tells a story and represents the talent of the region's native peoples. It is the perfect place to take a piece of this experience home.

Our visit to El Cortijo Hotel Boutique was, without a doubt, one of those experiences that remain etched in memory. A place where luxury is defined not by ostentation, but by authenticity, human warmth, and a deep connection with culture and nature. If you're looking to disconnect from the frenetic pace of the city and immerse yourself in the magic of the Calchaquí Valleys, El Cortijo is your ideal destination. We especially thank Vero Velardo from Hotel El Cortijo for making this unforgettable experience possible.
