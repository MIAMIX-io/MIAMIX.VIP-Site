Jacqueline Ceballos, celebrated as The Queen of Floral, graced the cover of SOMOS LA REVISTA, marking a remarkable midday celebration. The event, held at the prestigious Saks Bal Harbor store, was a tribute to the talent, elegance, and entrepreneurial vision of a woman who has turned her passion for flowers into a lasting legacy of beauty and inspiration.

The atmosphere was infused with the refined fragrances of Parfums de Marly, whose exclusive scents filled the space with sophistication and charm, perfectly enhancing the spirit of the occasion. Amid flashing cameras and joyful smiles, MIAMIX was on site capturing every magical moment of this unforgettable afternoon.

Guests enjoyed a fine selection of gourmet bites and carefully curated wines, offering a sensory experience that complemented the celebration flawlessly. Every detail was crafted to reflect luxury and exquisite taste values that Jacqueline Ceballos expresses in each of her floral creations through her brand, Petal Cartel.

A heartfelt moment came when the President of Parfums de Marly @parfumsdemarly offered warm words of congratulations to Jacqueline, praising not only her artistic talents but also her innovative and entrepreneurial spirit. He also shared a few behind the,scenes stories about the brand’s most iconic fragrances, creating an intimate and exclusive experience for the attendees.

As CEO of Petal Cartel petalcartel.com, Jacqueline has built a strong name in the world of luxury floral design. Her vision goes far beyond petals, each arrangement is a symbolic, elegant, and emotionally resonant experience. This is why she has been internationally recognized as The Queen of Flowers, a title she carries with grace, authenticity, and determination.
