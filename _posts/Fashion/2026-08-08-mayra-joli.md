# **MAKING**

**HISTORY AT MISS UNIVERSE DOMINICAN REPUBLIC**

Mayra Joli became one of the most captivating figures of Miss Universe Dominican Republic 2026, earning a place among the Top 20 semifinalists and receiving the special Miss Superación award, an honor that celebrates perseverance, determination, and the ability to inspire others through personal experience. At 60 years old, Joli did more than step onto a pageant stage; she challenged expectations and offered a powerful new perspective on beauty, confidence, and the meaning of representation.

![](/assets/images/Fashion/6E355285-F2E8-4107-B9FD-5FCF4873C8EA_4.png)

Her participation attracted national and international attention, making her one of the oldest contestants in the recent history of the competition. Yet her story goes far beyond age. Joli had participated in the pageant world decades earlier, before her family emigrated to the United States in 1990. Returning to the stage more than three decades later, she brought with her the experience of an accomplished life while carrying the same dream that had inspired her in her youth.

Her presence became part of a broader and positive conversation about the evolution of beauty pageants and the importance of celebrating women with diverse ages, backgrounds, experiences, and life journeys. In a culture increasingly redefining beauty, Joli represented the idea that confidence and ambition do not have an expiration date, inspiring women across generations to pursue their dreams regardless of how much time has passed.

![](/assets/images/Fashion/2013AB6D-AB7B-4921-9C6B-A4B5F143E388_1.jpeg)

Born in the Dominican Republic and based in Miami, Joli has built an accomplished career at the intersection of law, entrepreneurship, media, and public influence. As an immigration attorney, entrepreneur, communicator, and influencer, she has created a distinctive professional identity that combines achievement with purpose. Her journey has made her a recognized figure within South Florida’s Hispanic community and a prominent voice on issues affecting immigrants and families.

As the founder of Joli Law Firm, a Brickell based law firm dedicated exclusively to immigration law, Joli has established a strong reputation for advocating on behalf of immigrants and families seeking to create new opportunities and build their future in the United States. Her legal career has positioned her among the most visible professionals in South Florida’s immigration field, while her public profile has allowed her to connect professional success with a broader commitment to community.

![](/assets/images/Fashion/IMG_6132_0.jpeg)

That commitment is also reflected in her philanthropic work. Joli is also part of Fashion Night on Brickell, an annual event dedicated to raising funds for local charitable organizations, and has supported initiatives involving organizations such as Dade Legal Aid, Autism Speaks, Casa Valentina, and Miami Rescue Mission. Through these efforts, she has demonstrated that visibility can become a platform for meaningful action and that success carries an opportunity to give back.

Her presence across television, media, and digital platforms has further expanded her influence. Joli frequently uses her public voice to promote causes connected to social justice, inclusion, immigration, and access to opportunity, while also sharing her passion for fashion, lifestyle, travel, entertainment, and philanthropy. Her ability to move naturally between these worlds has created a multidimensional public persona that extends well beyond the traditional boundaries of a beauty pageant.

![](/assets/images/Fashion/94E0C444-D9B1-4CBB-841D-42A17A4A5947_3.png)

Her participation in Miss Universe Dominican Republic 2026 therefore represents much more than a moment of competition. Earning a place among the Top 20 semifinalists and receiving the Miss Superación distinction adds another remarkable chapter to a life defined by ambition, resilience, discipline, and reinvention. Her journey reflects a woman who has continued to evolve while remaining connected to the dreams that shaped her earliest aspirations.

In an era when the definition of beauty continues to expand, Mayra Joli stands as a powerful reminder that elegance is also about confidence, character, purpose, and the courage to begin again. Her story proves that dreams do not belong to a particular age or moment in life. Sometimes, the most inspiring chapter begins when the world least expects it, and Mayra Joli has shown that it is never too late to pursue a dream, break barriers, and open new paths for the generations that follow.
