Argentina carries a long, almost symbolic, wait: more than six decades since Norma Nolan gifted us our only Miss Universe crown in 1962. Since then, the desire to hear our country's name at the top again has become a dream that has spanned generations. A dream that seemed dormant… until now.

Because this year, that hope has a face, a story, a voice. And her name is Aldana Masset. Aldana isn't just the new Miss Argentina; she's that woman who comes along every few years to remind us that true beauty isn't a retouched photo, but an inner strength. A strength born from vulnerabilities, from personal scars, from the times life breaks you… and yet you still get back up. Perhaps that's why she's so moving: because she doesn't represent an unattainable ideal, but a truth.

Today she is among the top favorites in the pageant, within the Top 5 according to experts and Miss Universe fans. And no one gave her that position: she earned it through discipline, stage presence, and a quiet, elegant charisma. Furthermore, her victory has added significance: she is the first woman from Entre Ríos to be crowned Miss Argentina in over 70 years, opening a historic door for her province and bringing diversity to the national representation.

But what truly sets her apart is her story. Aldana spoke openly about her alopecia, about living with an insecurity that could have held her back. In a world that demands constant perfection, she chose to be authentic. That's where her strength was born. That's where the woman who now graces the international stage without asking permission, yet without losing her sensitivity, was born.

From my perspective, one that always maintains that true beauty comes from within, Aldana represents a generation that understands that beauty is also courage, sincerity, and purpose. She is the voice of all women who transform their fears into wings. She is proof that hope has form, character, and determination.

The competition will be fierce, yes. More than 120 women will give it their all on stage. But there's something about Aldana that transcends any technique or runway: her light. That light that can't be learned, it's innate. And Aldana possesses it.

None of this would be possible without the quiet, dedicated, and strategic work of the team that supports Aldana every day. Behind every rehearsal, every wardrobe choice, and every public appearance are professionals who believe in her as much as the entire country does. My special recognition goes to Jorge Ramírez, Adrián Malavé, and Dhey Galaviz, who sustain this dream with commitment, vision, and a deep love for what they do. Thanks to them, Argentina feels close, very close, to another crown.
