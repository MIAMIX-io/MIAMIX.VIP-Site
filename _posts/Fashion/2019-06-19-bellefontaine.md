Bal Harbour Shops was the setting for an exclusive event that brought together luxury, beauty, and fashion in one place. Carrie's at Neiman Marcus opened its doors to welcome more than 30 special guests for a unique day designed to celebrate the power of well-cared-for skin and the charm of iconic fragrances.

The evening's hosts, Fernanda Sosa and Stephanie Rosenberg, welcomed guests and led a sensorial experience that combined the best of Swiss skincare with the glamour of the fashion world. The event, titled "Champagne & Caviar," offered attendees the opportunity to discover BelleFontaine Switzerland's premium line, renowned for its signature caviar-enriched formula.

Guests were able to experience this new release for the first time and try it firsthand, completing the experience with a touch of haute perfumery.
