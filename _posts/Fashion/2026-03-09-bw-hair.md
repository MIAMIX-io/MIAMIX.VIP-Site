Located in one of Florida’s most exclusive areas, BW Hair Studio & Spa is much more than a beauty salon. It is a true refuge of style and well‑being. Founded in 2013 by Rubén and Claudia, an Argentine couple with more than 30 years of experience in the beauty industry, this space has become an icon for local and international clients who seek excellence, warmth, and results that stand out.

“We don’t sell just to sell,” says Rubén. “We want the client to trust us, feel comfortable, and return because they truly enjoyed the experience.” And they achieve it. Over the years, BW has welcomed Argentine celebrities and international artists who trust the team’s professionalism and the relaxed, friendly atmosphere. “They come from everywhere. Some seasons we see more Argentines, others more Europeans. There’s always movement,” he says with pride.
