To speak of masculine elegance is to speak of TOMMY’S TUXEDOS, an iconic house that since 1976 has dressed generations of gentlemen for the most special moments of their lives. With more than 45 years of experience, its name has become synonymous with distinction, personalized service, and meticulous attention to detail.

<!-- SINGLE IMAGE -->

TOMMY’S TUXEDOS

At the helm of the brand is LUIS CASELLA, CEO and a key figure of the brand, contributes a modern and dynamic business vision. Thanks to his leadership, TOMMY’S TUXEDOS has maintained its excellence and close connection with clients, becoming a trusted name in Miami and beyond.

By his side, ELIZABETH, wife and a true reference of refined taste on the most prestigious red carpets. She is the creative mind behind the looks that dazzle at events such as the Martín Fierro Latino Awards, Premios Lo Nuestro, and the sophisticated Paris Fashion Week, always bringing her expert eye that perfectly combines elegance and personality.

And of course, TOMMY, the man behind the brand’s name, remains the heart of the experience. His deep knowledge of cuts, fabrics, and proportions ensures that every client finds the perfect suit for their style and occasion. With his guidance, each choice becomes a statement of timeless elegance.

The TOMMY’S TUXEDOS experience goes far beyond simply renting a suit. With an extensive catalog of designs and accessories, personalized service, and professional tailoring, the boutique offers solutions for every need, from sizes up to XXL and options for children and young men, to same day service and cleaning included.

If you ever need a tuxedo at the last minute, their team is ready to respond efficiently and precisely, although reservations are recommended 10 to 15 days in advance. They also offer special pricing for long term rentals or for clients who are traveling.

In my own experience, I had the pleasure of being dressed by TOMMY'S TUXEDOS for the Martín Fierro Latino Awards twice. During the second edition, I had the honor of hosting the red carpet, an unforgettable moment where my look truly became part of the show. My all-black change of outfit that night remains one of the most memorable and celebrity-inspired experiences of my life. A gallery of that extraordinary evening follows below.

Having the guidance of people who genuinely understand the art of dressing well makes all the difference. Elizabeth is not only a fashion expert, she is a true professional with an intuitive sense of elegance, style, and proportion. Her ability to highlight each person's individuality while maintaining sophistication is what sets TOMMY'S TUXEDOS apart.

In addition, TOMMY’S TUXEDOS is our trusted partner for creating standout looks at various events across Miami, South America, and Europe. Their collaboration brings an extra touch of elegance and distinction to every occasion, ensuring that each appearance reflects sophistication, confidence, and timeless style.
With locations in Miami and Miami Lakes / Hialeah, TOMMY'S TUXEDOS continues to be the first choice for those seeking quality, tradition, and impeccable style.
