Splendor in the Garden celebrated its 15th annual edition, reaffirming its place as one of South Florida’s most anticipated afternoons where fashion and philanthropy seamlessly converge. Hosted at the iconic Fairchild Tropical Botanic Garden, the event brought together leaders from fashion, society, and the philanthropic community in an atmosphere of elegance and purpose.

Presented by 【entity-Neiman Marcus¦canonical_name=Neiman Marcus】 Coral Gables and sponsored by The Collection, the afternoon showcased The Best of 【entity-Neiman Marcus¦canonical_name=Neiman Marcus】 Spring 2026 collections, set against the garden’s lush and timeless tropical scenery. The natural surroundings elevated the experience, creating a refined yet organic stage for the season’s most coveted looks.

The curated presentations highlighted fresh silhouettes, vibrant seasonal hues, and lightweight textures that captured the spirit of Spring 2026. Fashion and nature existed in perfect harmony, offering guests an immersive visual experience that celebrated creativity and style in an open-air setting.

A meaningful highlight of the event was the recognition of Elizabeth Boulenger, Jessica Cristobal, Ashley Cusack, Linda Lynn Levy, Sarah Lewin, and Jocelyn Woolworth, honoring their dedication, leadership, and lasting contributions to the community and philanthropic initiatives throughout South Florida.

{% include gallery.html folder="/images/curated" images="IMG_4901.jpeg" cols=1 %}

The afternoon reached an emotional peak with the presentation of the Fairchild Icon Award to Gloria Estefan, whose extraordinary career and cultural influence extend far beyond music. Her commitment to philanthropy and community engagement continues to inspire generations locally and globally.

Notable attendees included Marile Lopez, Tatiana Guiribitey, Lissete Hermida, Adriana Vargas, along with members of the Miami Women Who Rock organization, among other prominent figures from Miami’s fashion, social, and charitable circles.

{% include gallery.html folder="/images/curated" images="IMG_4980.jpeg|IMG_4968.jpeg|IMG_4973.jpeg|IMG_4965.jpeg" cols=2 %}

Throughout the event, guests enjoyed moments of connection, conversation, and celebration, reinforcing Splendor in the Garden’s role as a vital gathering point for like-minded individuals passionate about style with purpose.

Beyond the glamour, the event underscored the powerful role fashion can play in driving positive change, supporting meaningful causes, and strengthening community bonds through shared values and collaboration.

{% include gallery.html folder="/images/curated" images="IMG_4976.jpg" cols=1 %}

Once again, Splendor in the Garden proved to be an essential fixture on Miami’s social calendar - where elegance, community, and charitable impact flourish together in a setting as inspiring as the mission it supports.
