Luxury took on a deeper meaning in Coral Gables during An Afternoon with Purpose, an intimate VIP experience that seamlessly blended elevated fashion with social impact. Hosted by Doral Genesis in partnership with the Out of Foster Foundation, the event unfolded inside the iconic Jimmy Choo boutique, transforming a refined retail space into a platform for connection, awareness, and giving back.

Set at Merrick Park, the Jimmy Choo Coral Gables boutique provided the perfect backdrop for an afternoon defined by elegance and intention. A carefully curated guest list ensured an atmosphere that felt both exclusive and welcoming, where every detail from the setting to the conversations, reflected a shared appreciation for craftsmanship, community, and conscious living.

From the moment guests arrived, the ambiance spoke volumes. Understated luxury, warm hospitality, and thoughtful design created an experience that felt personal rather than performative. It was a reminder that true sophistication lies not only in aesthetics, but in the values that shape an experience.

At the heart of the event was a meaningful collaboration between Doral Genesis and the Out of Foster Foundation. Together, they spotlighted a mission rooted in empowerment: supporting children and young adults impacted by the foster care system. The partnership demonstrated how brands can leverage their platforms to amplify causes that matter, without losing authenticity.

Throughout the afternoon, guests enjoyed a bespoke shopping experience while engaging with the foundation’s work on a deeper level. Conversations flowed naturally, blending fashion insights with stories of resilience, mentorship, and opportunity. The result was an environment where philanthropy felt accessible, personal, and genuinely inspiring.

The Out of Foster Foundation is a nonprofit organization dedicated to uplifting youth affected by foster care through education, mentorship, advocacy, and access to essential resources. Its mission centers on creating pathways to stability and confidence, ensuring that young people navigating life during and after foster care are met with support rather than obstacles.

By aligning with a globally recognized luxury brand and a respected local sponsor, the foundation expanded its reach while preserving its heartfelt message. The collaboration proved that impact-driven initiatives can coexist beautifully with high-end experiences, creating value that extends far beyond a single afternoon.

Jimmy Choo Coral Gables played a vital role in shaping the event’s success. The boutique’s sophisticated interiors and impeccable client experience encouraged exploration, dialogue, and connection. Guests moved effortlessly between discovering the latest designs and engaging with like-minded individuals who share a passion for purpose-driven living.

The event was made possible by Doral Genesis, a luxury automotive brand known for refined design, innovation, and an elevated client journey. Beyond its commitment to excellence, Doral Genesis continues to demonstrate leadership in community engagement, supporting initiatives that bridge luxury with meaningful social impact.

What made An Afternoon with Purpose truly special was its sense of intention. This was not merely a social gathering or a shopping moment, but a thoughtfully curated experience designed to foster awareness and generosity. As guests departed, they carried with them more than memories of a beautiful afternoon they left with a renewed belief that when luxury meets heart, real impact follows.
