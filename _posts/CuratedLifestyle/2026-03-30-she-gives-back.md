Where Style Meets Purpose

Founded by Jenisbel Acevedo, S.H.E. Gives Back offered something far more meaningful than just a shopping event: a space where style and purpose seamlessly intertwined. This purpose-driven experience, presented by Acevedo and Friends, redefined what it means to look good while doing good.

At its core, S.H.E. Gives Back is about connection, bringing together women who believe that empowerment extends beyond aesthetics.

It is an invitation to shop with intention, support a greater cause, and engage with a community of like-minded individuals who value impact as much as image.

Set against the backdrop of the Westchester Cultural Arts Center, the event created an atmosphere that felt both elevated and heartfelt. Guests were immersed in a curated selection of luxury and exclusive designer pieces, offering a unique opportunity to discover fashion that tells a story. Beyond the racks and displays, a deeper mission remained at the heart of the experience: giving back.

A portion of the proceeds benefited 【entity-St. Jude Children’s Research Hospital¦canonical_name=St. Jude Children’s Research Hospital】, an organization dedicated to advancing treatments and saving the lives of children facing serious illnesses. This partnership added a profound layer of purpose, reminding attendees that every purchase contributed to something far greater than themselves.

What set S.H.E. Gives Back apart was its ability to blend sophistication with sincerity. It speaks to a modern woman who understands that true empowerment is not defined by labels or price tags, but by impact, connection, and heart. It is about aligning personal style with personal values and recognizing that the two can coexist beautifully.

Throughout the day, attendees were encouraged not only to shop, but to engage. Conversations flowed, connections were made, and a sense of community unfolded naturally. It was this energy, warm, intentional, and inspiring, that transformed the event into more than just a shopping experience. It became a movement.

As a featured special guest, the experience offered a unique and intimate glimpse into the intention behind S.H.E. Gives Back and the meaningful connections it fosters.

S.H.E. Gives Back also highlights the growing shift within the fashion industry toward conscious consumerism. Today’s consumers are increasingly seeking brands and experiences that reflect their values, and initiatives like this answer that call. They serve as a reminder that fashion can be a force for good when approached thoughtfully.

Ultimately, S.H.E. Gives Back is about redefining luxury, not just as something you wear, but as something you feel and contribute to. It is the luxury of giving back, of supporting a cause, and of being part of a community that believes in making a difference.

For the women who attended, it was more than a day of style. It was a statement. A reminder that when purpose leads, everything else follows.

S.H.E. Gives Back. Style. Heart. Empowerment.
