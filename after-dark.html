The Host

Julio Medina

Cuban Author · Storyteller · Host of After Dark

Six worlds. One night. After Dark moves through fashion & runway, VIP parties & nightlife, luxury lifestyle, the celebrity & influencer scene, red carpet & exclusive events, and hotels, restaurants & destinations — one night, one story, one look.

From Chamboli, Havana to Miami, Julio turned survival into storytelling. Author of From Chamboli to Miami (2025) and Adictos, and host of 7 Values, he now tells the stories of Miami after dark.
